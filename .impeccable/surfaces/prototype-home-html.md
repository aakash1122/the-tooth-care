# Home page surface brief

Scope: home page, prototype at prototype/home.html, later the Astro index route. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: first-time visitor on a phone from Google Maps, Facebook, or a friend, often anxious or burned by a chain clinic, deciding whether to message this dentist.
Action: WhatsApp message to Dr. Uzzal; call is the quiet second action.
Proof: three verbatim Google reviews (first name, branch), two chambers with real phones and hours, ratings, the doctor's own words. Portrait, logo, signature, degrees line ship as labelled stand-ins.

Chosen direction: The Prescription Pad. The home page is a note written to you on Dr. Uzzal's letterhead pad.
Memorable moment: the red rubber stamp "Message Dr. Uzzal on WhatsApp" pressing into the paper, the only action colour on the page.
Sequence, one claim per sheet: the note, three review slips, first visit as Rx lines 1 to 3, services as the pad's tick-list of questions, two chamber cards, a short letter with the signature slot, back cover footer.
Disciplines: portrait at eye height in viewport one, one signal colour, one claim per section, fixed measure from the ruled lines, next step always visible (sticky pad strip on mobile).

Unresolved: exact hours, degrees and BMDC number, which reviews the owner wants named, WhatsApp routing through the edge redirect in the Astro build.
Owed to the Astro build: self-hosted subset fonts (Vollkorn, Alegreya Sans, Archivo Narrow); the prototype loads them from Google Fonts.
