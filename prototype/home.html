How your first visit goes

Rx

  1. We talk. You tell me what worries you, what happened last time, and what you want to avoid. I listen before I look.

  2. I explain. I show you what I see on the screen, in plain words, and give you the cost range before anything is decided.

  3. We decide together. If the right decision is to do nothing today and think it over at home, that is a good visit too.

A note from me

Most people who sit in my chair for the first time are not really here about a tooth. They are here because the last visit somewhere else hurt, or was rushed, or ended with a bill they did not understand. I know that, so we start slowly.

I will tell you what I see and what I would do if it were my own mouth. Sometimes that is a filling. Sometimes it is nothing yet. I would rather you come back in a year and trust me than leave today with work you did not need.

If you have been avoiding the dentist for years, you are in good company here. Nobody is going to make you feel bad about it.
