# Home page surface brief

Scope: home page, prototype at prototype/private-practice.html, later the Astro index route. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an everyday visitor on a phone from Google Maps, Facebook, or a friend, deciding whether to contact this practice. Must recognise a dental practice at a glance.
Action: WhatsApp message; call is the second action.
Proof: real Google review fragments quoted verbatim (names to confirm), two chambers with real phones and hours, the named lead dentist. No ratings or review counts are shown anywhere (owner decision, 2026-09-23). Photos, degrees, BMDC number, opening time, associate names ship as labelled stand-ins.

Chosen direction: The Private Practice. Near-white ground, light display serif headings, hairline rules, logo navy reserved for actions and emphasis rules, large warm photography. Premium and modern, not homely.
Copy register: plain clinic language, no slogans (owner decision, 2026-09-23). Hero is a short factual headline, one sentence, two actions.
Sequence: hero, first appointment (three steps), patient quotes, treatments index, dentists, chambers, closing contact panel, footer. Sticky Message and Call bar on phones, hidden while the hero button is on screen.

Rejected in this round: The Prescription Pad (prototype/home.html) and Signature Blue (prototype/signature-blue.html); kept on disk as evidence only.
Unresolved: exact hours, degrees and BMDC number, reviewer first names, associate names, WhatsApp routing through the edge redirect in the Astro build.
Owed to the Astro build: self-hosted subset fonts (Libre Caslon Display, Schibsted Grotesk); the prototype loads them from Google Fonts.
