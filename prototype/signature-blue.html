How a first visit goes

Most fear of the dentist is fear of not knowing what happens next. So here is exactly what happens.

  1. 1

    We talk

    You tell us what worries you, what happened last time, and what you want to avoid. We listen before we look.

  2. 2

    We explain

    We show you what we see on the screen, in plain words, and give you the cost range before anything is decided.

  3. 3

    We decide together

    If the right decision is to do nothing today and think it over at home, that is a good visit too.
