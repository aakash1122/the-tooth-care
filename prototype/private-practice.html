Your first appointment

What happens when you come in, in the order it happens.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    You describe the problem and your dental history. If you are anxious or had a bad experience elsewhere, tell us; it changes how we work.

  2. 2

    Examination and explanation

    We examine your teeth, take an OPG X-ray if needed, and explain the findings on the screen in plain words, with the cost range for each option.

  3. 3

    Treatment plan

    You decide what to do and when. A consultation on its own is a normal visit.
